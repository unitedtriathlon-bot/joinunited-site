About Ivan

A strong start. Room to grow.

Ivan coaches across swimming, cycling, running and triathlon, with functional training supporting the work in each discipline. His practice includes both children and adults — not just cycling or kids’ sessions.

Coaching experience

With 7+ years of coaching and 200+ athletes coached, Ivan also coaches the St Petersburg Triathlon Federation team. Children can join group training from age 7, with individual work available from age 3.

Athletic background

Ivan holds Candidate for Master of Sport qualifications in both cycling and triathlon. His university specialization in cycling coaching adds a technical foundation to his work across endurance sport.
