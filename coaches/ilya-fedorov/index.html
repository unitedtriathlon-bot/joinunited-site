Athlete → coach → founder

Built from inside the sport.

Ilya grew up in competitive swimming before moving into triathlon, where swimming, cycling and running became one system rather than three separate sports. That athlete's perspective is at the core of his coaching: understand the person, build the right structure, improve the details and make training sustainable enough to produce a result.

As Founder and Head Coach of UNITED, he works both with athletes preparing for triathlon races and with people who want to become stronger, technically better and more confident in endurance sport.
