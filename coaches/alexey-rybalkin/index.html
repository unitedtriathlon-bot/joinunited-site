About Alexey

Learn from the race.

Alexey brings a former professional cyclist’s perspective to training. Technique, endurance, race preparation and equipment choices belong together: the rider and the bike need to work as a system.

Coaching experience

His coaching focuses on cycling technique, racing and performance development, with support for bike fit and equipment setup. Riders can work on the skills and decisions that matter to their own goals.

Athletic background

Alexey’s international racing background includes the Giro d’Italia and podium finishes in stage racing and championship competition.

  • Youth Tour de France — general classification bronze
  • European Championship — individual race bronze
  • Giro d’Italia participant
  • Volta a Portugal — youth classification winner
  • Tour of Romania — 2nd general classification
  • Vuelta a Madrid — youth classification winner
